Your work.

MyTaskDock is a local-first productivity workspace that brings tasks, deadlines, files, notes, and communication history together. Each task becomes a persistent workspace, so important context doesn't disappear when the work is done.

Task-centered workspace

Organize deadlines, notes, instructions, and related information around the work itself instead of scattering them across apps.

Files stay with the work

Drag and drop related files into each task and keep the complete working context together from start to finish.

History that doesn't disappear

Preserve messages, decisions, and task history instead of losing them when a sticky note or temporary to-do item is deleted.

AI that understands the task context.

We're exploring Claude-powered features for summarizing task history, extracting action items from messages, organizing information, and helping users quickly resume complex work without rebuilding context.
